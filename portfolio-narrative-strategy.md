**Part 1 --- Website Structure Recommendations**

Before rewriting words, the architecture of the scroll matters. Here\'s
the recommended flow --- and the reasoning behind each decision.

**Recommended Section Order (Scroll Flow)**

**1 → Hero (The Hook)**

First impression in under 5 seconds. Bold headline, one-liner identity,
single CTA. No clutter.

**2 → About (The Origin)**

Your story starts here. Who you were before → what drove you → who you
became. This makes you human, not just a skill list.

**3 → Craft / Skills (The Arsenal)**

Move Skills BEFORE Projects. Visitors need to understand your toolbox
before they can appreciate your work. Show skills as confidence, not a
resume checklist.

**4 → Projects (The Proof)**

Now skills have context. Each project is a mini case study: problem →
solution → result. Filter tabs stay. Cards need stronger copy (see Part
2).

**5 → Vision (NEW --- The Future)**

A short, forward-looking paragraph that shows ambition. This section
elevates you from \'available for hire\' to \'someone building toward
something\'. Add this as a standalone section or fold it into the footer
above Contact.

**6 → Contact (The Invitation)**

End with warmth and clarity. The CTA is not a formality --- it\'s the
goal of the entire page.

**3 High-Impact UX Changes to Implement**

-   Add a subtle animated typing effect to the hero subtitle (e.g., \'I
    build · I ship · I solve\') --- creates a dynamic first impression
    without heavy animation cost.

-   Replace generic skill progress bars with named tech badges or a
    visual icon grid. Dots/bars feel abstract; logos (Kotlin, Flutter,
    Firebase) feel real and scannable.

-   Add a \'Currently Building\' or \'Open to Opportunities\' pill badge
    near the hero name --- recruiters scan for availability signals
    instantly.

-   Pin the floating nav: make it shrink/minimize on scroll down and
    expand on scroll up for cleaner reading experience.

-   Add micro-copy under the contact form (\'Typically replies within 24
    hours\') --- reduces friction and builds trust before the send
    button is clicked.
