## Scroll Flow Recommendations

### 1. **Progress Bar**
- ✅ Implemented. Gives visitors a sense of journey completion.
- Consider: subtle color shift as they approach Contact (e.g. accent intensifies).

### 2. **Chapter Transitions**
- ✅ Chapter dividers between sections create visual "scene changes."
- Consider: subtle parallax or fade on dividers as they enter viewport.

### 3. **Staggered Reveals**
- ✅ Reveal animations on scroll keep the eye moving.
- Consider: slightly longer delays on key lines (e.g. hero description, vision quote) so they land with more weight.

### 4. **Mobile: Condensed Challenge**
- On small screens, the Challenge list could collapse into an expandable "What I solve" accordion to reduce scroll length.

### 5. **Sticky CTA**
- Consider: a floating "Let's talk" button that appears after ~50% scroll, linking to #contact. Dismissible. Keeps the CTA accessible without breaking the narrative.

### 6. **Nav Shrink on Scroll**
- Pin the nav and make it shrink/minimize on scroll down, expand on scroll up. Reduces visual noise during reading.

### 7. **Micro-copy Under Contact**
- ✅ "Typically replies within 24 hours" — reduces friction.
- Consider adding: "No spam. No automated responses." (already in copy)

## Content Tone Checklist

- [x] **Hook:** Bold, specific, not generic ("I build mobile apps that people actually use").
- [x] **Beginning:** Human, relatable, shows the "why."
- [x] **Challenge:** Concrete problems, not abstract skills.
- [x] **Craft:** Tools as trust, not checklist.
- [x] **Proof:** Each project = mini case study (problem → solution → result).
- [x] **Vision:** Ambitious but grounded. Partnership, not desperation.
- [x] **CTA:** Confident, warm, specific ("If you're building something ambitious, let's talk.").
