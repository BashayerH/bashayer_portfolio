01 The Origin

How I Became a Mobile Developer

It started with a bootcamp that changed everything.

My journey into development began at the Tuwaiq Academy bootcamp, where I built my first complete application and truly understood the fundamentals of programming. That experience sparked a deep curiosity about how digital products are built.

After graduating, I started working on small projects that strengthened my skills and gave me real experience solving practical problems through code، My first professional role introduced me to Flutter, where I spent a year and a half building cross-platform mobile applications and exploring a new side of mobile development.

Later, I returned to my foundation: Kotlin. Since then, I’ve been continuously refining my expertise in Android development, improving my architecture skills, and building applications that are reliable, scalable, and designed to last.
